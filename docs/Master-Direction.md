# Hason Platform — Master Direction v1.0

The permanent creative direction. Overrides stylistic assumptions; read before any experience work. Architecture rules remain governed by CONSTITUTION.md and the approved blueprints.

## What we are building

A digital conference experience platform — not a website, not a landing page, not a CMS front. The conference starts online; the visitor should feel they have already arrived before they physically attend.

## North star

We direct an experience, not pages. One continuous journey; the visitor moves through an event, never between sections. Reveal — don't explain.

## Experience DNA — five emotional stages

Place → Purpose → People → Journey → Decision.

| Scene | Chapter | Emotional goal |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Arrival | hero | "I want to be there." |
| 02 Purpose | story | "I understand why this matters." |
| 03 Experience Flow | agenda | "I can imagine my day." |
| 04 People | speaker-grid | "I trust the people behind this event." |
| 05 Venue | venue | "I know where I am going." |
| 06 Join | registration-cta | "I am ready to attend." |

Every scene: one purpose, one visual hero, one emotional objective, one transition into the next. No scene competes with another.

## Laws

- **Place before interface.** Photography before UI; atmosphere before interaction. Never reversed.
- **Design philosophy**: editorial, architectural, quiet, confident, premium, timeless. Never trendy, flashy, or overloaded. Learn from magazines, museums, architecture, hospitality — imitate no one.
- **Visual language**: photography is the interface; typography creates authority; whitespace creates rhythm; motion creates continuity; color creates atmosphere; decoration creates nothing.
- **Rhythm**: no two consecutive scenes feel identical — alternate image/text emphasis, wide/narrow, centered/offset.
- **Continuity**: transitions through spacing, composition, color, scale, photography — never animation as a patch.
- **Motion**: soft fade, gentle reveal, subtle parallax, micro-interactions only. Never heavy parallax, scroll hijacking, complex timelines, canvas animation. Motion should almost disappear.
- **UI**: no cards everywhere, no heavy borders, no large shadows, no dashboard feeling. Numbers are typography, never widgets. People are faces, never profile cards. The day is a journey, never a calendar or table. Venue is story, not logistics. Registration is the emotional conclusion, never the first ask.
- **Mobile**: recompose, never resize.
- **Non-negotiables**: performance (LCP, CLS, hydration), accessibility (reduced motion, keyboard, screen readers, semantics, heading hierarchy, touch targets, RTL/LTR), clean code, docs in /docs only.

## Experience Profile (per event, future)

Venue type, atmosphere, photography style, accent style, motion intensity — these change the feeling of an event, never the platform.

## The two final questions

After every implementation: Does this feel like one continuous experience? Would someone remember it tomorrow? If not — keep refining.
